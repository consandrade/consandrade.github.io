## Research

I currently work with two research groups.

At **Rice University**, I occasionally work with the Morosan Lab, conducting experimental condensed matter physics research. My project focuses specifically on the magnetic properties of rare-earth tellurides.

At the **University of Houston**, I work with Dr. Gunaratne and collaborators in theoretical biophysics. My current research focuses on understanding and characterizing why fat-tailed distributions emerge so frequently in relevant biophysical systems.
